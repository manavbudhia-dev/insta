Privacy Policy

This is a private, self-hosted tool used by its owner to manage messages, comments and posts for the owner's own Instagram professional account. It is not offered to the public.

What data is processed

When you message or comment on the connected Instagram account, this tool receives, through the official Instagram API, your Instagram-scoped user ID, your username and display name where available, and the content of your message or comment.

How it is used

The data is used only to reply to you and to keep a conversation history for the account owner. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage and retention

Data is stored on a server controlled by the account owner. Delivery records and logs are deleted automatically after 60 days; conversation history is kept until the owner removes it.

Deleting your data

To have your data removed, send a direct message to the connected Instagram account asking for deletion. Your records will be erased on request.
